Motivation

When designing ontologies, assessing whether they capture required data without introducing inconsistencies is critical.

Producing synthetic data that exercises the ontology is time-consuming—especially across multiple iterations of complex or evolving data models.

Research gap

Existing LLM-based ontology tools focus primarily on ontology design support.

OWL-SDA takes a data-driven evaluation approach: generate and validate synthetic data rather than rely on LLMs to design the ontology.

OWL-SDA

An open-source multi-agent tool that uses AI agents to efficiently generate and validate synthetic data expressed in OWL.

Evaluation

Demonstrated on two domain-specific ontologies:

  • Industrial emissions reporting (RIE-IEPR, Flemish Government)
  • Healthcare ontology (UZ Brussel)

Results

OWL-SDA successfully surfaced inconsistencies and missing relations in both ontologies across multiple generation iterations.

Conclusion & Future Work

  • OWL-SDA automates synthetic data generation for ontology evaluation
  • Multi-agent validation loop reduces manual effort
  • Future: support for SHACL constraints, larger ontologies, benchmarking
